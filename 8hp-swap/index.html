Straight answers

8HP swap questions we get.

Which 8HP should I use?

They all share the ZF 8HP architecture, so what matters is torque rating and ratios. The 8HP45 and 8HP50 handle a stock-turbo car; hybrids and E85 want an 8HP70 or 8HP75; a big single wants the 8HP90/95. Dodge 845RE/850RE/8HP70 units are cheap and everywhere, Jaguar and Maserati 8HP70s are the same box with a different bellhousing, and the BMW units bolt up. Gen 1 ratios (4.71 first, 0.67 eighth) and gen 2 (5.00 first, 0.64 eighth) feel different: try both above.

Does the swap actually make the car faster?

Same engine, same tires: yes, mostly from the shift. A 6HP or a manual loses 0.3 to 0.4 seconds every gear change; the 8HP loses about 0.15 and has two more gears to keep a turbo engine in its plateau. The simulator shows 0.2 to 0.5 s in the quarter on a typical N54, more on a car that was shifting a slow automatic before. Gearing-wise the real gain is 60–130.

Do I need to change the diff?

Often not. The 8HP's 0.67 or 0.64 eighth is taller than a 6HP19's 0.69 or a 6MT's 0.85, so with your stock diff the cruise rpm drops. Ask the assistant for the optimal setup and it scores the common BMW diffs against your engine and tires.

Can I run an 8HP behind a non-BMW engine?

Yes, that is most of the swaps now. LS, Coyote, 2JZ, VQ, RB, Hemi and the AMG V8s all go in front of an 8HP with an adapter plate and flexplate; the Dodge units bolt straight to a Hemi and the BMW units to a BMW. Pick your car above, or the Custom entry and type your ratios: the physics does not care what badge is on the hood.

What else does the swap need?

A standalone transmission controller or a BMW DME/TCU that speaks to the 8HP, the right bellhousing and flexplate, a driveshaft, mounts, a trans cooler and a shifter solution. The gearbox is the cheap part. If you want it done right the first time, text the shop.
