What we do on these

The work

B58 power

Flex fuel, downpipe, intercooler, charge pipe, tune. Then hybrid or big single turbos for the cars that want more. We build Supras on the same bench.

N55 turbo

Stock replacement or upgraded turbo, oil lines, wastegate rattle fixes, and the oil filter housing gasket while the front's apart.

Tuning

MHD and Bootmod3. Custom maps, logged and revised until it's right.

Reliability

Valve cover, OFHG, coolant, VANOS solenoids, charge pipe. The known stuff, fixed before it strands you.
